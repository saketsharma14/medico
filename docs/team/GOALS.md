# Goals

_Shared by the whole team. Update when goals or milestones change._

## Project vision
A white-label chronic-care platform (B2B2C) that pulls a patient's scattered records into one timeline, keeps them on schedule with tests and prescribed medication, and gives the payer or clinic proof that it worked. First condition: type 2 diabetes. Markets: India (prove the product) and UAE (prove the business).

Full plan: [`docs/plan/`](../plan/) — start with `00-brief.md`, then `01-brief-review.md` and `02-founder-inputs.md`.

## Out of scope (for now)
- Any feature that recommends or changes medication (clinical safety line)
- Real patient data in the UAE before in-country hosting and legal sign-off
- Real booking/pharmacy integrations, teleconsultation, device sync (after pilot)
