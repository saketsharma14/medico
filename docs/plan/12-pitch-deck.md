# Deliverable 10: Pitch Deck Outline

_Version 1, 2026-11-19. Two decks: an investor deck for grants, incubators and pre-seed, and a shorter partner deck for sales calls._

> **No statistic in this outline is filled in.** Wherever a slide needs a number, the outline says what the number is and where to get it. Don't present a figure you haven't sourced; investors and medical directors both check.

## 1. Rules for both decks

- One message per slide, stated in the headline as a full sentence. A reader who sees only the headlines should get the whole argument.
- Use your own evidence over market reports: interview quotes, beta numbers, a real extraction on screen.
- Every external figure carries its source and year in small print.
- Say plainly what you have and haven't proved. Overclaiming is the fastest way for a student team to lose a room.
- No medical claims. The product "helps members stay on schedule"; it does not "improve diabetes" until pilot data says so.
- Keep the investor deck to 12 slides plus appendix; the partner deck to 8.

**Appendix slides** (shown only if asked): architecture and security; regulatory map and gates; care-gap rule set and sources; extraction accuracy method and results; pilot design with comparison group; unit economics detail; risk register; roadmap to 18 months.

### Version by stage

| Stage | Slides that carry the weight | What's honest to say |
|---|---|---|
| **Now** (incubator and grant applications) | 2, 4, 5 (mock-up or spike), 6, 12, 13 | "Pre-product. Here is the problem evidence we're gathering, the plan, and why we can build it." Replace slide 10 with "What we'll prove in 90 days". |
| **After discovery and R1** (Feb–Mar 2027) | Add real 5 and 10 | Interview count and quotes; extraction accuracy; demo |
| **Pre-seed** (mid 2027) | 10 and 14 lead | Beta retention; signed pilot |
| **Seed** (mid 2028) | 10 becomes two slides: pilot outcomes versus comparison; pipeline | Outcome data; contract; customer #2 |

### Questions investors will ask

Prepare a one-paragraph answer to each; put supporting detail in the appendix.

- [ ] Why would an insurer pay when members switch every year?
- [ ] What stops an insurer, a hospital group or a government app from building this?
- [ ] Why will patients use an insurer-branded health app?
- [ ] How accurate is extraction, and what happens when it's wrong?
- [ ] Are you a medical device? What licences do you need?
- [ ] Two students: who sells to insurers, and who is clinically accountable?
- [ ] Why two markets at once?
- [ ] How long is the sales cycle, and how do you survive it?
- [ ] What happens to the business if exchange access is never granted?
- [ ] Who owns the intellectual property, given you're at a university?

## 3. Partner deck (sales calls, 8 slides)

Sent after a first call, or used in the demo meeting. The demo itself matters more than the slides.

| # | Slide | Headline (example) | Content |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Your situation** | "You know which members have diabetes, but not which ones are falling behind this month" | Their problem in their words, taken from the discovery call |
| 2 | **What we do** | "We find members who are off track, bring them back, and show you the result" | Three steps: identify, engage, report |
| 3 | **For your members** | "A simple app under your brand that keeps all their reports and reminders in one place" | Three phone screens; languages; privacy promise |
| 4 | **For your care team** | "A daily worklist instead of a spreadsheet" | Worklist screen; filters; outreach log |
| 5 | **For your management** | "A report your actuary can accept" | Sample outcome report; comparison-group method in two lines |
| 6 | **Safety, privacy and security** | "No medical advice, data stays in-country, your staff see only what members allow" | Clinical safety line; firewall; security summary; documents available on request |
| 7 | **The pilot** | "Six months, [N] members, fixed fee, clear success measures" | Scope, timeline, metrics and targets, what we need from you |
| 8 | **Next steps** | "Three things to agree this month" | Cohort and data; sponsor and care-team lead; security review start date |

Leave-behind: a two-page PDF version of the pilot proposal from the go-to-market plan.

## 4. Demo script (5 minutes)

Use synthetic data only.

| Minute | Show | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 0–1 | Patient opens an invitation and signs up | "Two minutes, and the first screen says what the insurer can and can't see." |
| 1–2 | Photograph a lab report; values appear; one flagged for confirmation | "It reads the report twice by different methods and asks the patient to check anything uncertain." |
| 2–3 | Timeline and HbA1c trend; overdue-test nudge with source | "Every item shows where it came from. The nudge cites the guideline and says to discuss with the doctor." |
| 3–4 | Care manager's worklist; filter overdue kidney screening; log a call | "This is who to call today, and why." |
| 4–5 | Outcome report with comparison group | "And this is what you show your management at month six." |
