## What this changes

A two-person student team with no budget and no contacts cannot run regulated enterprise pilots in two countries at once. Doing both markets is still workable if each market gets a **different job**:

| | India | UAE |
|---|---|---|
| **Job** | Prove the product | Prove the business |
| **Why** | Cheaper to operate, open health-record sandbox (ABDM) **[Verify]**, huge variety of lab report formats to harden extraction, easier access to early users | Mandatory insurance, payers that carry chronic-care cost, higher price per member, the team is based here |
| **First 6 months** | Founder-run beta with 50–100 consenting diabetic users (family, community, one friendly clinic). Measure extraction accuracy, activation, retention. | Discovery interviews, incubator entry, find a clinical advisor and a design partner. No patient data handled until hosting and legal basics are in place. |
| **First paying customer** | Diabetes clinic chain or employer wellness programme (small ticket) | Mid-size clinic chain or TPA pilot first, insurer second |
| **Cost to start** | Low: cloud free tiers, India region hosting | Higher: in-country hosting, regulatory opinion, security review. Fund through grants and incubator credits. |

### Consequences for the plan

1. **Insurers stay the target buyer, but the first pilot will probably be a clinic chain, TPA or employer.** Insurer sales take 9–18 months **[Assumption]** and need security reviews a student team can't yet pass. The brief already allows a clinic chain as design partner; use that route to get evidence, then sell to insurers with it. Insurer discovery interviews still start in month 0.
2. **The upload-and-extraction pipeline is the first thing to build.** It needs no partner, works in both countries, and produces the demo.
3. **Three gaps to close before any pilot:** a clinical advisor (a practising endocrinologist or diabetologist, equity or advisory terms), a commercial advisor with payer experience, and an incubator that provides legal and cloud credits. Candidates to check: the university's own incubator, Hub71, in5, and India's university and government startup schemes. **[Verify]** which are open to student founders.
4. **No real patient data in the UAE until** in-country hosting and a lawyer's sign-off exist. Demos use synthetic data.
5. **Spending rule:** nothing is paid for until a grant, credit or customer covers it. The financial model will show a zero-cash path to the first pilot.
6. **Region portability (review item 1) is now mandatory,** not optional: two deployment cells (India, UAE) from the start, same codebase.
